## About the course

Learners keep the books of Orchid Bay Trading Pte Ltd, a fictional Singapore SME, in QuickBooks Online Plus: configuring the company, importing master and banking data, running the sales, purchasing, banking and inventory cycles, coding and reviewing GST, and producing the reports an SME owner needs. All names and financial records are mock data for training only.

The slide deck is concept-led and visual. Detailed click-by-click procedures, evidence requirements and acceptance criteria live in the Learner Guide and the activity packs.

## Learning outcomes

- **LO1:** Configure and import relevant data to the QuickBooks accounting system.
- **LO2:** Input and control day-to-day sales, purchasing, banking and inventory data in QuickBooks.
- **LO3:** Compute and review GST for expenses and invoices using appropriate Singapore tax codes.
- **LO4:** Generate and interpret financial, accounting, ageing and GST reports for SME decisions.

## Topics covered

1. **Introduction to QuickBooks Online Plus Accounting System**
2. **Input Data to QuickBooks**
3. **Manage Goods and Services Tax (GST)**
4. **Financial and Accounting Reports**
